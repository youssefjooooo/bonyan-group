Bonyan Group - About Us
Who We Are
Bonyan Group is an Egyptian industrial supply company serving construction, manufacturing, and heavy industry across Egypt and the Middle East. We specialize in steel materials, lifting and crane solutions, copper products, and industrial coatings, supporting projects that demand strength, precision, and reliability.
Our work goes beyond supplying products. We operate as a supply partner that understands how material quality, delivery timing, and technical compatibility directly affect project success.

Our Approach & Values
Integrity, reliability, and commitment to quality define how we work. We believe trust is built through performance, not promises. Every material we supply represents our name on a project site, which is why we focus on meeting specifications, maintaining consistency, and supporting our clients beyond the point of sale.
We prioritize long-term partnerships by delivering dependable solutions and clear communication at every stage.

Our Mission & Vision
Our mission is to provide high-quality industrial materials and lifting solutions that support the growing construction and industrial sectors in Egypt and the Middle East, while meeting international standards for safety and performance.
Our vision is to become a trusted regional industrial supply partner known for consistency, technical understanding, and long-term cooperation.

Why Bonyan Group
Clients choose Bonyan Group for our multi-division capabilities, reliable sourcing, and practical industry experience. We combine product knowledge with operational awareness, ensuring that what we supply works in real conditions, not just on paper.
Our goal is not to be the largest supplier, but the most dependable one.
